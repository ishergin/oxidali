A line is born UNRESOLVED (…) and settles: an activation reaches the browser on its own channel a few tens of milliseconds after the event, so «no rule matches» is only honest once that window has passed. Asserting it immediately is what the screen used to do, and it read as fact while the device was firing rules the whole time. An instance type the product does not model still shows its raw ten bits rather than the word «event»: an unmodelled type is precisely the case where the raw word is the only thing an operator can act on. The no-rule line is the commonest symptom of a mistyped rule, and it doubles as DiiA's own recommended way to identify a device — press the button you are about to name. Client lag is one counter, the sniffer's dropped_since shape: there must not be two different ways of being behind.
