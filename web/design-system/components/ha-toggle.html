HA toggle

Per-entity Home Assistant opt-in (ha_entity_enabled / ha_select_enabled). A metadata affordance in the info tint — it must never compete with lamp state, which is what the amber accent is reserved for. Default is on; unchecking retracts the entity from the broker (empty retained config), so the switch does something an operator can see.

States

exposed (default)
excluded
bridge disabled (settings)

Where it appears

Virtual lamps tableper-lamp column switch (virtual-lamps.html)
Group header popoverbeside the rename field (group-matrix.html)
Scene headerbeside the scene name (scene-editor.html)

Label is always the two words “Home Assistant” — no icon, no abbreviation: the checkbox is small enough that the words are the affordance. Uses accent-color: var(--info); the checked state must not read as “the lamp is on”.
