Green is live, amber-of-the-warn-hue is reconnecting (and polling meanwhile), red is more than 60 s without a connection. The lamp accent --accent is never used here.

Refused is not Offline

Offline means nobody answered and the ladder is still climbing. Refused means the controller answered at once and said no for a reason that cannot change while the page is loaded — today only ws_origin_rejected, i.e. this page was not served by the controller. Nothing is retrying behind it: the dot is hollow, it does not pulse, and the caption says screens poll instead rather than reconnecting. Retrying a permanent refusal cost a handshake, an error frame and a close every 15 s per tab, all of it through the controller's single httpd task.

ws_clients_exhausted deliberately stays Reconnecting: a client slot frees the moment another tab closes, so that one is transient.
