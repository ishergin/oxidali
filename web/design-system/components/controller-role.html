Active, or a lone controller — deliberately nothing
◍ dali2rust
Dashboard

DALI sniffer

No badge for the active unit, and that is the design. Every build sends X-Dali2rust-Role on every response, so a badge that also appeared for active would be a permanent, unvarying caption on every single-controller installation — the kind nobody reads and everybody stops seeing. What an operator has to know without being told is the case where their writes will be refused.

The same fact on the OLED, where a word does not fit
192.168.13.140A5D05H
GEAR 12/12IN 3/3
192.168.13.140S5D05H
GEAR 12/12IN 3/3

Twenty-one columns against a fifteen-character address and an uptime of five to seven leave room for a letter, not a word — and the letter goes in FRONT of the uptime because an overlong pair loses its tail, so what survives a clip has to be the fact no other surface carries. A standby also inverts the row: every other row of that screen reads as a broken controller (no gear driven, no HCL, no poller), and it is what makes one letter enough.

The badge reads the response header rather than polling /api/v1/redundancy, which is why it stays live on screens that know nothing about redundancy. That is the whole reason the role travels as a header instead of a field in forty DTOs.
