Nothing measured yet

A dashed ring, and the word is not observed rather than a colour. Before the first frame the PHY has classified nothing, and green would be a claim about a measurement that has not been taken — the same rule that keeps unknown distinct from off in the lamp-state cell.

With the load figure (2026-09-05)

The verdict says whether the wire is alive; the figure at its right says how much of it is in use, and own beneath it how much of that was us. It is the device's own one-second gauge, the same number Stats and the OLED show. The counts strip trades sys failure — already in the verdict's detail line — for foreign, the sniffer's forward-frame count. A dash and not measured until the PHY has booked a tick; the history strip is in components/bus-load.
