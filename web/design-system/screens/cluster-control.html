Home is the node that issued the record: this node, or node k for a record read from that node's announcements (cached). A record whose home node went silent (stale) keeps its last announced name under home node k silent; its number fades in Nodes. A record whose home node is not heard at all is shown from the copy this node keeps for its own part, under home node k not heard.

State says how many members of the channel are lit, of those whose state is known, and never a level: a channel has no level of its own. The dot is filled when some node reports its part on, empty when every member is known to be dark, and dashed otherwise. Members not known are said in words after the count, with the nodes that are not heard: 3 of 5 known lit · 4 of 9 not known · node 2 not heard. A part whose link cannot be checked gives no members to the count and is named as a part: 1 part not known. A node not heard is named even when every counted member is known, since its own members may not be counted; a part on a node behind the node that owns its bus is not counted, as on the controller. Lit members that no node knows to have been switched on through the channel leave the state unknown, as on CH19: after a restart a node does not remember being commanded. A stale or copied record keeps its row with the state not known, and a channel with no member reads no lamps, never off.

On, Off, the steps and the level act at once on every node that accepts. − and + send one DALI step down or up; there is no hold-to-dim, as on the product's group controls. The slider holds no position: releasing it sends that level once, and from the keyboard the arrows move it and Enter sends. node k ignores: a node takes part with Accept off. A refusal stays under its row, in words, until the next command to that channel: cluster_busy when four steps already wait for the channel, and the causes of a cluster that is off or not running. The row CH0 · deleted channel is this node's part that still names a record deleted on its home node: it gets no commands, and Finish delete removes it in two presses. The row CH40 · channel not heard yet is this node's part naming a record whose home node has not been heard, so whether the record lives is unknown: Unlink frees its group in two presses. A node whose parts cannot be read is named after the nodes of every row, node 3 not read, rather than drawn as taking no part.
