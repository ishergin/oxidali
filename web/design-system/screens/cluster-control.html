Home is the node that issued the record: this node, or node k for a record read from that node's announcements (cached). A record whose home node went silent (stale) keeps its last announced name under home node k silent; its number fades in Nodes. On, Off and the level act at once on every node that accepts; the slider holds no position, because a channel has no level of its own until nodes report their lamps (stage I6-C): releasing it sends that level once, and from the keyboard the arrows move it and Enter sends. node k ignores: a node takes part with Accept off. A refusal stays under its row, in words, until the next command to that channel. The row CH0 · deleted channel is this node's part that still names a record deleted on its home node: it gets no commands, and Finish delete removes it in two presses. The row CH40 · channel not heard yet is this node's part naming a record whose home node has not been heard, so whether the record lives is unknown: Unlink frees its group in two presses. A node whose parts cannot be read is named after the nodes of every row, node 3 not read, rather than drawn as taking no part.
