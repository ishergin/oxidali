Not the bank-206 rows

The Diagnostics tab shows rows with the same words — Short circuit, Open circuit, Thermal shutdown — from DiiA 253 memory banks. Those carry a condition with history: whether it has happened, when, and how often. These are the Part 207 queries: one instantaneous byte each. A gear may answer one family and not the other, so neither can stand in for the other, and the card titles have to make that unmistakable at a glance.
