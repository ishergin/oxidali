frames/s is a one-second window swept twice a second, not a per-batch figure: the controller does not flush an empty batch, so a rate recomputed only on arrival froze at the last burst and read as a busy bus for as long as the bus stayed silent. It decays to 0, and Stop and Clear zero it. The showing newest count appears only when the view is windowed.

Follow keeps the newest row in view. Scrolling up switches it to Manual and freezes the window — rows keep arriving into the buffer, but the view stops moving under the line being read; the footer then counts them as newer. Scrolling back to the bottom follows again. Pause is the other axis: Follow decides where the view sits, Pause decides whether rows are collected at all.

A stamp that reads +2.139 is time since boot, not a time of day: the controller has no RTC battery, so after a reset its clock sits at the epoch until SNTP anchors it a few seconds later. Rendered as a clock those records would show a convincing 03:00:02 in a UTC+3 browser — and they are exactly the records someone reads, because they are the ones from the seconds after a flash. Hovering says why.
