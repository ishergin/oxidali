2026-09-05 — how busy, and with whom

The bus card answered "is the wire alive?" and nothing about how much of it is in use. The PHY interrupt now books every 104 µs tick as idle, active or transmitting, a one-second window on the device turns that into ONE per-mille gauge, and every surface — this card, Stats, the OLED strip, the WebSocket frame — reads that same number; the browser keeps a five-minute ring of the pushed samples and the device keeps no history at all. Spec in components/bus-load.

Three things on the card changed. The verdict block gained the figure at its right, with our share beneath it — the gap to the total is the other master and the gear answering us, which on this bench is the question. The counts strip trades sys failure (it lives in the verdict's own detail line) for foreign, the sniffer's forward-frame count. And the history strip sits under the counts: total load as an --info area, our share as the neutral dashed line the HCL curve uses for colour, collisions as red ticks at the moment they happened. Never amber — amber is lamp state, and a filled amber area under the verdict would read as light output.

2026-09-01 — the bus is the first card

The dashboard used to answer "is the bus alive?" with a small bus idle chip on the adapter card, and that chip was never about the bus: bus_status on the adapter DTO is enabled ? "idle" : "disabled", a property of the adapter's own command gate. It reads "idle" over a segment whose supply has failed and over one shorted end to end. The wire's own condition now leads the page — IEC 62386-101 §4.11 Table 4's classification, straight from the PHY — and the adapter card's chip says what it actually knows (enabled / disabled). Five states, spec in components/bus-status.

Also gone: the Wi-Fi SSID row. The board has no radio — ADR-011 deleted the WiFi NetworkLink with the S3 — so the row could only ever render a dash, and a field that can only be empty teaches an operator to distrust the ones that are.

2026-09-01 — the shell grows, and folds

The shell was capped at 1240 px since the first screen, which is why tables looked truncated on a wide monitor: the device list needs ~1150 px of columns inside a 1020 px content area. It runs to 1680 px now — the widest surfaces here are the group matrix (17 columns) and the scene editor, and a table is the one thing that should use the glass it is given. Below 900 px the 200 px sidebar becomes a horizontal strip along the top and .grid2 collapses to one column, so the tables get the width the furniture was holding.
