Display

The OLED status panel on the controller: whether it is lit, how it is wired and mounted, and which pages it turns through.

Panel

On by default. Off powers the panel down and draws nothing; the controller keeps running.

The two SSD1306 addresses; the jumper on the panel module decides which one it answers. The wrong one leaves the panel blank.

180° for a panel mounted upside down on the rail. Portrait is not offered: the 21-column summary does not fit a panel 64 pixels wide.

Off, or 10 s … 24 h. After this long with nothing new to show the panel powers down; a new action other than an HCL step or a background read, a button or slider, an operation, a failure or a live fault (link down, bus alarm, faulty gear) wakes it.

s

Pages

At least one. The panel shows each selected page for 5 s, always in this order; with one page it stays put. Summary: address, devices, bus, HCL, services, last input, live action. Network: IP, role, uptime, time sync, MQTT, WebSocket clients, flash save state. Bus: bus verdict, frames and foreign frames per minute, wire load, poller, gear counts, load histogram. Activity: running operation, last action with its source and age, last input event, HCL.

How long each page holds the panel before the next one. Not configurable.
